Common Questions

Questions We Get Asked Most

Honest answers. No marketing language.

How long does it actually take to see results?
+
Most people notice improved skin hydration within 2–4 weeks. Visible changes to skin firmness and fine lines typically appear between weeks 4–8. Hair and nail improvements often become noticeable by week 6. Collagen is not a fast fix — it's a cumulative process. Think of it like going to the gym: you don't see abs after one session, but consistency over 8–12 weeks produces real, measurable change.
What makes Korean Marine Collagen different from regular collagen?
+
Korean marine collagen comes from deep-sea fish, which produces a collagen peptide with smaller molecular size and higher bioavailability than bovine (cow-based) or standard marine sources. Our peptides are nano-hydrolyzed to under 1,000 Daltons — meaning they're absorbed directly into your bloodstream rather than being broken down further during digestion. This is why we see faster results compared to larger-molecule supplements.
Can I mix it in hot drinks like coffee or chai?
+
Yes. Hydrolyzed collagen peptides are heat-stable — the hydrolyzation process has already broken them into smaller fragments that don't denature further with heat. It dissolves completely in hot liquids without changing the flavor or texture of your drink. Coffee, chai, warm water, soups — all work fine.
Is this suitable for vegetarians or vegans?
+
No. Marine collagen is animal-derived (from fish), making it unsuitable for vegetarians and vegans. If you follow a plant-based diet, we recommend exploring our Vitamin C + Zinc formula which supports your body's own collagen synthesis using plant-based ingredients.
Will it cause weight gain?
+
No. This product contains zero added sugars, zero fats, and no carbohydrates. Collagen is a lean protein. At 5g–10g per serving, it adds roughly 20–40 calories to your day — far below anything that would contribute to weight gain. It's also compatible with keto and low-carb diets.
